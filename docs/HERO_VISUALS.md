# EducationOS AR concept visuals

These original fictional scenes were generated with the built-in image generator in transparent-background mode, then encoded as WebP at quality 82. The visible glasses are an imagined augmented-reality learning concept. AR glasses are not available in the current demo; this is stated beside the five public-page hero visuals and in the interactive subject gallery.

| Placement | Asset | Story and prompt focus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | `hero-ar-physics.webp` | Learner in transparent AR glasses studies a three-dimensional Earth and orbital/force overlays |
| Programs | `hero-ar-programs.webp` | Learners of different ages explore a cell, a geometric solid and an exploded robot |
| Approach | `hero-ar-approach.webp` | Learner compares two cardboard prototypes with a spatial assembly model |
| Trust | `hero-ar-trust.webp` | Learner and caregiver study a real bridge alongside an AR cross-section |
| Journal | `hero-ar-journal.webp` | Learner traces a visual programming flow beside a physical robot |
| Gallery | `world-ar-programming.webp` | Robot program loop and changing output |
| Gallery | `world-ar-investing.webp` | Simulated choices, diversification and risk with no real money |
| Gallery | `world-ar-anatomy.webp` | Heart model and circulatory pathways, without gore |
| Gallery | `world-ar-ocean.webp` | Reef ecosystem, species and water relationships |
| Gallery | `world-ar-aviation.webp` | Paper glider, wing cross-section and airflow |
| Gallery | `world-ar-marketing.webp` | Two posters for a community tree-planting campaign |
| Gallery | `world-ar-business.webp` | Recycled desk-organizer prototype and production steps |
| Gallery | `world-ar-languages.webp` | Two learners connect speech, objects and meaning without invented words |

Shared prompt direction: realistic, diverse fictional learners; visible transparent AR eyewear rather than obscuring headsets; empathetic study actions with physical objects; detailed and coherent 2D annotations plus spatial 3D models; EducationOS white, black and lime with restrained cyan; high-key editorial lighting; no logo, unreadable interface text, fake progress metrics or product claims. The gallery is an interactive visual preview: subject buttons change the image and explanation, and a separate link leads to the real paper-bridge demo.
