# EducationOS learning visuals

The user-provided EducationOS slides informed the visual direction: warm white space, black type, lime and blue accents, dimensional learning objects, and a few child-centred scenes. These three assets are original generated illustrations, not copies of the slides or photographs of real EducationOS learners. They are illustrative; no AR eyewear, physical robotics kit, learning score, or production AI feature is implied.

| Asset | Use | Prompt intent |
|---|---|---|
| `public/images/curiosity-hero.jpg` | Public homepage hero | Fictional curious child on a warm-white background imagining a globe, book, paper plane, tactile geometry and friendly robot; inclusive, age-appropriate, editorial light, lime/blue/lavender; no words, logos, real child identity or device UI. |
| `public/images/learning-objects.png` | Public bento and inner-page banners; portal headers and lab cards | Isolated transparent 3D constellation of recognizable globe, open book, folded plane, fraction disc, lime cube, beads and friendly robot; premium toy materials, clear silhouette, no text, logos or people. |
| `public/images/maker-studio.jpg` | Public approach banner and learner Today visual | Fictional child at a bright table working with a small toy robot; abstract visual diagrams of loops, geometry and nodes; no readable code, brand, screens or claim of functional AR hardware. |

Generated bitmap assets are kept local and served from `public/images`. The learning activities, diagrams, 2D fallback and progress controls are native HTML/SVG/CSS, so imagery can be removed without removing lesson meaning or input affordances. Marketing and portal share only the visual vocabulary and static assets; they remain separate routes and production services. Generated images never represent actual learners, actual measured outcomes, or current product hardware.
